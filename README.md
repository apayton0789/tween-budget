Purpose: Give the Tween L.E.A.D. program director clear, at-a-glance visibility into exactly how much they can spend in Year 1, with full line-item detail and notes on demand.

How to Open & Use

Open the file
Double-click  in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari). It works completely offline — no internet or installation needed.
Four main tabs (top navigation)
At a Glance — Big Year 1 total ($32,550), key insights, and visual charts showing where the money is allocated.
By Category — Six clickable cards (Travel, Field Trips, End of Year Celebration, Food & Prep, Program Supplies & Equipment, Student Basic Needs & Incentives). Tap any card to open a detailed modal with every line item and the original notes.
Year 1 Monthly Planning & Ramp-Up — Shows the monthly burn rate, which items ramp up in the second half of the year (month 7+), and planning guidance for cash flow.
Full Line Items — Searchable and filterable table of all 19 line items. Use the search box or category dropdown to quickly find anything. Copy visible rows as CSV if needed.

Interactive features
Click any category card → opens a clean modal with Year 1 authority highlighted first.
Use the Copy category summary button in any modal to paste exact numbers and notes into an email or PO request.
In the Full Line Items tab, use the search box and category filter. The “Copy visible as CSV” button exports what you see.
Print button (top right) produces a clean printable version.
